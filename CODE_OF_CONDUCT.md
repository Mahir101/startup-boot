# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Harassing speech or behavior
- Discriminatory or derogatory comments
- Posting inappropriate images or screenshots
- Personal attacks of any kind

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening an issue or sending an email to the repository maintainer.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action to keep the community safe.
